Change your rules: what’s instant and what waits

Stricter applies at once, easier waits a day, and switching a site off takes a short quiz. Here’s what falls where, and why it works that way.

You can change any QuizBlock setting whenever you like, but not every change applies straight away. Anything that makes QuizBlock stricter is instant. Anything that makes it easier waits until tomorrow, and switching a site off takes a short quiz and a pause.

Why it works this way

You set QuizBlock up with a clear head. The moments you’d most like to undo it are the ones it’s there for: late at night, halfway down a feed, when one more video feels urgent. If relaxing a rule took one click, a weak moment could undo a decision you made on a good day. A day’s wait, or a few questions, gives the urge time to pass. If you still want the change tomorrow, it’s yours.

Instant: anything stricter

  • Adding a site, or switching one back on
  • Blocking a site completely
  • A stricter setting: Gentle to Balanced, or Balanced to Strict
  • Fewer free minutes (free time already given today shrinks to match)
  • A harder level
  • A lower daily limit for a site
  • A longer pause between sessions
  • Adding focus hours

Tomorrow: anything easier

  • A gentler setting
  • More free minutes
  • An easier level
  • A higher daily limit for a site, or No limit
  • A shorter pause between sessions, or none
  • Shorter focus hours

When you pick an easier option, the control shows it outlined and a note appears right below, such as “Gentle from tomorrow. Relaxing a setting waits a day; tightening is instant.” The change applies after midnight; until then, your current setting holds. To cancel it, click the button in the note (for example Keep Balanced) or choose your current option again.

Quiz subjects, the theme and the language aren’t stricter or easier, so they change at once.

Switching a site off or removing it

On the Sites page, the switch beside each site turns its gate on or off, and Remove is in the site’s details (click the site to open them). Switching a site off, removing it, or taking a blocked site back to challenges goes like this:

  1. Say why. QuizBlock asks what’s going on. For some answers it suggests a lighter change instead, such as dropping the daily limit from tomorrow.
  2. Answer the questions. Get 15, 20 or 25 right on Gentle, Balanced or Strict. QuizBlock picks them. Leaving partway keeps the gate on, and the same questions wait for you that day.
  3. Wait 20 seconds. “You earned it. One last pause.” If you’ve changed your mind, Keep the gate is one click.
  4. Confirm. Click the final button, such as Switch off YouTube.

Going the other way is instant: switching a site back on, adding it again or blocking it again. A site that’s already switched off can be removed straight away.

While a focus lock runs, or while focus hours cover a site, you can’t switch that site off, remove it or unblock it until they end.

Blocking a site completely

Setting When you visit to Block completely is instant, and also blocks any parts of that site on your list. Going back to Ask for a challenge takes the same questions and pause as switching a site off.

Parts of a site must be stricter

You can list a subdomain or a part of a site on its own, like old.reddit.com or youtube.com/shorts, but only if it’s stricter than the site that already covers it. Under a gated site, that means adding the part with Block completely. A part of a site that’s already blocked is covered as it is. Block part of a site has the details.

The same rule everywhere

  • During a focus lock, nothing can be made easier, not even for tomorrow. Changes already waiting hold until the lock ends, and while focus hours run.
  • Settings sync: when you turn sync on and another computer’s settings come in, anything looser than what you have here starts tomorrow.
  • Restoring a backup only adds sites or makes them stricter; more relaxed settings in it wait until tomorrow.

Backups and sync covers the last two.
