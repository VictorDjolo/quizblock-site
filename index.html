How it works

About two minutes of learning between you and the feed.

  1. Open a site you’ve blocked

    Instead of the feed, QuizBlock shows what it takes to get in: find 10 countries on a map, or get 8 questions right.

  2. Win the challenge

    It takes about two minutes. There’s no skip button, and reloading the page won’t give you an easier one.

  3. Your minutes start

    The site opens for 10 minutes. The countdown sits on QuizBlock’s toolbar icon and only runs while the site is open. At zero, it locks again.

  4. Watch what you know grow

    Countries you find colour in your map, anything you miss comes back until it sticks, and a weekly recap shows how often you walked away instead.

Built to actually work

Most blockers are easy to give in to. QuizBlock makes you earn every minute.

  • No skip button

    A site stays locked until you’ve earned your way in.

  • Daily limits

    Cap your total time on each site, however many challenges you win.

  • Changes of heart wait a day

    Tighten your rules instantly. Loosening them only takes effect tomorrow.

  • Focus hours

    Block everything on your list while you work, on a schedule or for a set time.

  • Only the worst parts

    Block YouTube Shorts or Instagram Reels and keep the rest of the site.

  • Hard to switch off

    Turning a site off takes a quiz and a 20-second pause. Turning it back on is instant.
