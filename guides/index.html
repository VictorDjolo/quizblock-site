Guides

How QuizBlock works, and how to set it up the way you want: sites, challenges, your own flashcards, focus hours and your data.

  • Getting started with QuizBlock Install QuizBlock, run the one-minute setup, and find your way around the gate, the toolbar popup and the dashboard.
  • How challenges and unlocks work What happens at the gate: the challenge QuizBlock picks, the minutes it earns, free minutes, daily limits and the three strictness settings.
  • Add your own flashcard decks Turn a CSV, TSV or Anki deck into questions QuizBlock asks in its quizzes, next to its own subjects or on their own.
  • Block YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels and other parts of a site Add a path like youtube.com/shorts or a subdomain to your list, and see how parts of sites, subdomains and sister domains are matched.
  • Change your rules: what’s instant and what waits Stricter applies at once, easier waits a day, and switching a site off takes a short quiz. Here’s what falls where, and why it works that way.
  • Focus hours and focus locks Set weekly focus hours, start a focus lock that can’t be cut short, and use groups so each one covers just the sites you choose.
  • Back up and sync your QuizBlock data Settings can follow you between computers; progress stays on each one. Export a backup to keep it, restore one safely, or start your learning over.
  • Import Anki decks with pictures and cloze cards Import .apkg files with pictures and cloze cards, choose which cards come in, and let QuizBlock quiz you on your own cards alone.
